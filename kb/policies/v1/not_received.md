## POL-NR-01: Goods or services not received
Applies to: not_received
If the customer paid for goods or services that were not delivered, and the customer has already contacted the merchant without resolution, the transaction amount is refunded as a provisional credit while the chargeback is processed.

## POL-NR-02: Merchant contact required first
Applies to: not_received
Before a not-received dispute can be accepted, the customer must have attempted to resolve the issue with the merchant. If they have not, the agent asks the customer to contact the merchant first and to come back if the merchant does not respond within 15 days.
